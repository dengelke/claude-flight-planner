## Daylight — the real constraint (timezone-free)

What matters is the **physical daylight window between dawn at Forrest and dusk at Tyabb**, not clock/timezone
arithmetic. Flying **south-east compresses daylight**: Tyabb is ~17° east (sun sets ~68 min earlier in absolute
time) *and* ~7° further south (shorter autumn days). Mid-April window:

| Window | Duration |
|---|---|
| Forrest **sunrise → Tyabb sunset** | **10 h 07 m** |
| Forrest **first light → Tyabb last light** | **10 h 58 m** |

Day 2 elapsed time needed vs that window:

| Day-2 route | Needs | Margin (sunrise→sunset) | Margin (first→last light) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **4 stops** (via Horsham) | 9 h 46 m | +21 min | +72 min |
| **3 stops** (Pt Augusta→Tyabb direct) | 8 h 48 m | +79 min | +130 min |

**Takeaway:** Day 2 is a **genuine dawn-to-dusk day.** Depart Forrest at first light; do not plan a leisurely
start. The 4-stop route only has real margin if you use legal twilight both ends — the 3-stop route buys ~1 h
of daylight comfort (at a fuel-margin cost, see Day 2).

### The Port Augusta → Tyabb decision (Leg 5/6) — DEFAULT TO THE HORSHAM STOP
Port Augusta → Horsham → Tyabb is **505 nm**; the direct Pt Augusta → Tyabb is **503 nm** — Horsham sits
**2 nm off the straight line**, so it costs nothing to keep as an in-track fuel stop.

**The direct leg is fair-weather-only.** Against the POH's fixed **10.1 USG reserve** (45 min @75% + descent),
even a still-air 503 nm direct leg lands with only **+3.6 to +5.6 USG of true spare** — and it busts the
reserve at modest headwinds:

| Headwind | 65% (145 kt/10.5): fuel on landing | 75% (152 kt/11.6): fuel on landing |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 kt | 15.7 USG (**+5.6**) ✓ | 13.7 USG (+3.6) ✓ |
| 10 kt | 13.0 USG (+2.9) ✓ | 11.0 USG (+0.9) ✓ |
| 15 kt | 11.5 USG (+1.4) ✓ | 9.5 USG (**−0.6 BUST**) |
| 20 kt | 9.8 USG (**−0.3 BUST**) | 7.9 USG (−2.2 BUST) |

**Reserve-bust headwind: ~13 kt at 75% power · ~19 kt at 65% power.**

**Go/No-Go rule for flying direct (skip Horsham):**
- Forecast **AND** observed headwind **< 15 kt**, **and** landing fuel projects **> 10.1 USG**, **and** full tanks ex Port Augusta → may overfly Horsham direct to Tyabb.
- **Fly the direct leg at 65% power** (145 kt / 10.5 GPH) — lands with ~2 USG more than 75%, buying ~6 kt more headwind tolerance.
- **Any doubt, any headwind ≳ 15 kt, or any weather/airspace delay → LAND HORSHAM**, splash fuel, continue (~30 min, zero distance).
- Note: 65% is slower, so a headwind also stretches the leg time and eats the tight daylight margin — another reason a real headwind means "stop at Horsham," not "power through."
Decide on **observed** winds overhead Horsham, not forecast hope.
