## Daylight — on our side this time (westbound, summer)

Unlike an eastbound trip, flying **west** *gains* clock time, and **Nov/Dec = long days**. The zone maths:
- **Day 1 (QLD → SA):** QLD has no DST (AEST +10); SA is on summer DST (ACDT +10:30) → only ~**½ h lost** — daylight essentially preserved.
- **Day 2 (SA → WA):** SA ACDT +10:30 → WA AWST +8 (no DST) → you **gain ~2.5 h of clock time** crossing into WA.

So the heavier Day 2 (~8 h airborne) is a non-issue — you land Busselton in the **early-to-mid afternoon local time** with hours of daylight to spare. **No dawn-scramble required** (the opposite of the eastbound YTYA plan). Still: start reasonably early, pull actual sunrise/sunset, and keep a buffer.
