# Tecnam P2002 Sierra — aircraft data card

> Transcribed from the **Tecnam P2002 Sierra Flight Manual, rev 4.00** (`.context/attachments/cRiWHq/p2002_poh.pdf`).
> Empty weight is airframe-specific — **read your aircraft's current Weight & Balance record** before flight.

## Powerplant & fuel
- **Engine:** Rotax 912 ULS, 98.5 hp (73.5 kW)
- **Fuel type:** **Mogas preferred** — auto fuel **min RON 95 (US AKI 91 premium), ethanol-free**; or **AVGAS 100LL** (prolonged 100LL use → more valve-seat wear / combustion deposits)
- **Specific gravity:** ~0.72
- **Capacity:** 2 × 50 L = 100 L total · **99 L / 26.2 USG usable** = **~71 kg (157 lb)** full

- At **330 kg empty**, full fuel (71 kg) + a 165 kg payload = **566 kg, 34 kg under MTOW** ✓.
- General rule: full fuel + a 165 kg payload fits MTOW while empty weight ≤ ~363 kg.

## Centre of gravity (datum = propeller support flange)
- **Forward limit:** 1611 mm / **63.4 in** (20.0% MAC)
- **Aft limit:** 1789 mm / **70.4 in** (33.0% MAC)
- **Loading arms:** occupants 70.86 in · baggage 86.61 in · fuel 60.23 in
- Note: the POH sample chart mixes unit conversions — compute in one unit set and confirm against the airframe W&B record.
