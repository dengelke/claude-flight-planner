**🔴 SEVERE — mid-air / traffic (fatal, hard to see or predict):**
- **Active parachute drop zone (PJE ops) over the bays** — canopies and a climbing/running jump aircraft in your circuit airspace, dropping *through* circuit height from above where you're not looking. Zones: Bickley Bay (094°M/0.8 nm), Salmon Bay (230°M/1.2 nm), Thompson Bay (066°M/0.6 nm). **Monitor CTAF 126.0 for jump calls; be ready to hold.**
- **High fly-in traffic volume on fine days** — Rottnest is a popular destination; expect many aircraft, non-standard joins, and a busy circuit. Continuous lookout + clear radio.

**🟠 HIGH — you control it if you read it / plan it:**
- **RWY 09 RIGHT-HAND circuits required in daylight** (non-standard) — get this wrong and you're nose-to-nose with circuit traffic. **Keep all circuits south; do not overfly the settlement on the N side.**
- **Approach obstacles:** **hill 79 ft infringes RWY 27 approach** (092°M/0.57 nm); trees infringe **RWY 27 approach by 8 ft**; **train on the railway at the S border can encroach the W end of RWY 09**; a bush infringes **RWY 09 approach by 3 ft**. (RWY 27 LDA is only **1121 m** vs 1293 m — displaced for these.) Also lighthouse 282 ft (270°M/1.9 nm) and lit wind turbine 289 ft (303°M/1.25 nm) to the W.

**🟡 MEDIUM — usually survivable / reactive:**
- **🦘 Wildlife:** quokka (marsupial) + migratory birds on and near RWY 09/27. For dispersal outside hours, contact AD MGR.

**🟢 ADMIN / nuisance — plan ahead, won't hurt you:**
