# Plan 1 — Busselton → Kalgoorlie → Busselton (via Northam, return through Boyup Brook)

**Route (2-day, Northam YNTM fuel both ways):**
- **Day 1 (out):** YBLN → **YNTM** → YPKG — *sleep at Kalgoorlie*
- **Day 2 (back):** YPKG → **YNTM** → **Boyup Brook** → YBLN
**Aircraft:** 2 × [Tecnam P2002 Sierra](../../aircraft/P2002.md) on the return · Rotax 912 ULS · MTOW **600 kg** · 99 L usable
**Planning basis:** No wind · Cruise 100 KTAS · Burn **20 L/hr** (conservative; POH ~15–18) · **final reserve 10 L (30 min, day VFR)**
**Loading (per aircraft):** occupants **170 kg** + cargo **20 kg** = **190 kg** · **full fuel at each fuelled departure**
**Data source:** ERSA FAC/RDS effective **09 JUL 2026** — verify against current ERSA + NOTAMs on the day.

> ✈️ **Bottom line:** Fuel at **Northam (YNTM)** both ways — sealed field, **AVGAS H24 self-serve card bowser, no PPR**, so
> it's the reliable splitting point. Every fuelled leg departs full and lands with 45+ L, so wind is never a range problem
> (longest leg 250 nm). **On Day 2 two aircraft fly home together; at Boyup Brook one stays for its annual and everyone
> consolidates into a single aircraft** for the short 50 nm hop to Busselton — well under MTOW because it's flown on a small
> fuel load (see W&B). It's an **AVGAS trip** (Northam and Kalgoorlie are AVGAS); fill Mogas at Busselton home base on Day 1
> if you prefer, but there is **no Mogas en route**.

## Fuel plan — AVGAS at Northam both ways (every fuelled leg departs full)

| Departure | Fuel type | Depart | Leg | Land with (nil wind) | Over 30-min reserve (10 L) |
|-----------|-----------|-------:|-----|---------------------:|---------------------------:|
| YBLN | Mogas or AVGAS | 99 L | → YNTM 140 nm | ~69 L | +59 L |
| YNTM | **AVGAS (H24 card)** | 99 L | → YPKG 250 nm | **~47 L** | **+37 L** |
| *— sleep Kalgoorlie —* | | | | | |
| YPKG | AVGAS 100LL | 99 L | → YNTM 250 nm | **~47 L** | **+37 L** |
| YNTM | **AVGAS (H24 card)** | 99 L | → Boyup → YBLN 183 nm | **~60 L** at YBLN | +50 L (Boyup has no fuel) |

- **Wind is not a range issue** — every leg departs full; the longest (250 nm) still lands ~31 L even in a 25 kt headwind.
- **Northam is the fuel stop** — sealed 14/32 (1248 m), **AVGAS H24 credit-card bowser** (Viva/Dunnings), no PPR, so no
  phone-ahead. If it were ever unavailable, the nearest AVGAS is **Jandakot (~50 nm SW, controlled)**.
- **Fuel type:** AVGAS at Northam and Kalgoorlie (no Mogas en route). The 912 ULS runs on 100LL fine; fill **Mogas at
  Busselton** home base on Day 1 if you want the 912's preferred fuel for that first leg.
- **Boyup Brook has no fuel** — from a full Northam tank the 183 nm to Busselton (via the Boyup stop) lands ~60 L.

## Day 2, two aircraft — Boyup Brook annual drop & consolidation

- Both P2002s fly **YPKG → YNTM → Boyup Brook** together (loose trail, individual CTAF calls; agree spacing/joining).
- **At Boyup Brook one aircraft stays for its annual.** Everyone consolidates into the other aircraft; **anything over the
  20 kg baggage limit stays with the parked aircraft**.
- The remaining aircraft flies the **50 nm Boyup Brook → YBLN** leg (see the final-leg W&B below).

- **591 kg < 600 MTOW ✓** (9 kg margin); CoG 68.4 in full → 69.5 in dry, inside 63.4–70.4 ✓. Valid **only if empty ≤ 339 kg**
  — at the POH-sample 339.7 kg you'd be ~1 kg over, so carry ~2 L less or trim cargo. Confirm the real empty weight.

**Final leg Boyup Brook → YBLN (50 nm) — the consolidated aircraft, even at 355 kg empty:**

| Fuel aboard | 355 kg empty + 2 pax (170) + 20 cargo | MTOW | CoG |
|------------:|--------------------------------------:|:----:|----:|
| Full 99 L | 616 kg | ❌ over | 68.4 in |
| ~60 L (as arrived) | ~588 kg | ✅ under | 68.7 in |
| 30 L (ample for 50 nm) | 567 kg | ✅ under | 69.1 in |

- **MTOW is not a limit here — because you're light on fuel.** 50 nm needs ~12 L + reserve, so don't top up beyond need;
  keep cargo ≤ 20 kg and **do the actual W&B**. The 330 kg airframe is fine even brim-full (591 kg). Being light also helps
  the takeoff off Boyup Brook's ALA.
